## Example 02: Emotional Framework Recognition Ladder

### Status

- Type: source-method example
- Purpose: preserve the four-step framework recognition method for short-drama analysis
- Default inheritance: partial
- Replace before reuse: exact source plot shell, source character names, and any platform-specific phrasing

### What This Example Covers

- one-line story capture
- episode-event chain extraction
- hook direction inspection
- framework reuse through surface replacement

### Framework Scan Steps

1. Compress the whole story into one sentence that names the initial state, the core event, and the final state.
2. Write the first major arc as an episode-event chain instead of a scene list.
3. Check the ending hook of each episode and ask what it is trying to pull the audience toward.
4. Identify the repeated emotional pattern and classify the framework.
5. Rebuild the story by swapping characters and incidents while keeping the underlying emotional frame.

### Common Signals

- repeated humiliation and recovery points to an uplift or revenge frame
- repeated misunderstanding and emotional escalation points to a tragedy or spiral frame
- repeated forced proximity and outside obstruction points to a romance-pressure frame
- if every hook asks about exposure, the framework is likely reveal-driven
- if every hook asks about relationship outcome, the framework is likely relationship-driven

### What To Preserve

- the idea that framework recognition comes before detail copying
- the use of a fixed sentence pattern for quick structural compression
- the focus on hook direction as a diagnostic tool
- the separation of emotional frame from surface plot

### What To Replace Before Reuse

- exact source drama examples
- any one-to-one plot mapping
- assumptions about one genre being the only useful frame
