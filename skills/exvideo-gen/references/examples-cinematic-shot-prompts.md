### What To Preserve

- The eight-part shot-design formula.
- The bilingual vocabulary as a fast selection library.
- The worked-case pattern that converts selected terms into a coherent prompt.
- The principle that character and environment content should be added separately.

### What To Normalize Before Reuse

- Separate shot size from composition.
- Separate camera angle or view from camera movement.
- Treat wide angle as a lens choice.
- Treat Dutch angle as framing or angle rather than movement.
- Use standard forms such as `backlight`, `360-degree orbit`, and `wide-angle lens`.
- Add narrative purpose, subject action, duration, and continuity before production use.
