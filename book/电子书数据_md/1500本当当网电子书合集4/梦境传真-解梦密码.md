# 8.Dream of Flying

【Dream】

I always dreamed of flying when I was in secondary school. In my dreams, I soared into the sky with my feet. I flied so high that the sceneries were too small for me to notice. Accompanied with the wind, I felt the flight so comfortable and a little sad as well. I had dreamed like this continually for almost 5 or 6 years. It is strange that I haven’t dreamed of flying in recent years. It’s a pity that I am not able to experience it again though I’d like to genuinely. However, I wonder why I felt complacent and comfortable, at same time, slightly uneasy. 

\--Female, postgraduate of arts, 23 years old

【Freudian Interpretation of Dreams】

【Revelation】 Teachers and Parents Should Face Teenagers’ Instinct

Why have the dreams of flying bothered the dreamer for so long? The dream seems related with adolescent libidos. The dreamer is a girl that desired for freedom, however, restrained by moral principles. I suppose she is exquisite, romantic and focuses on her own emotional experience due to her major of art. She attempted to raise questions about love in her dreams and obtained her own comprehension by thinking about the dreams.

Nowadays, with the rapid development of society, teenagers’ psychological maturity falls far behind their physical one. The lack of support and education from family and society will inevitably results in their panic or confusion about sexual desire that caused by natural growth of sexual organs.

Teachers and parents should pay more attention in instructing teenagers to face their instinct. It is the right way to transfer as many libidos as possible to other interests.

【Interpretation】 The dream reflected dreamer’s fancy of isomerism in her puberty.

The imagery of flying is freedom, confidence, expectation, releasing pressure, overcoming difficulties, etc. The flying dream indicates the dreamer desires for freedom and unshackled. “I soared into the sky with my feet”means the dreamer can fly comfortably and with an easy grace. Despite this, why did the dreamer feel slight uneasy? I suppose, with the increasing height she reached, the dreamer felt very unrealistic because the sceneries were too small for her to notice; she felt disturbed and lonely because she was apart from relatives and friends. The dreamer was in a dilemma: she yearned for freedom but feared that she might lose herself if given too much freedom.

Additionally, the pleasure and satisfaction of flying is similar to sexuality and that’s why flying dreams are always related with sex. With their physical maturity, it is normal for teenagers that sex fantasies become frequently. Having this kind of dreams can be considered as their sexual fantasies are partially met in the dreams. On one hand, her curiosity of opposite sex urged her to seek better sense of reality; on the other hand, the real sexual experience was beyond her cognition as she was only a secondary school student. This is the reason that the higher she flied, the more unrealistic she felt and it probably formed her feeling of sad, uneasy and lonely

Last but not least, the dreamer hasn’t had flying dreams recently. I guess that perhaps she has a boyfriend in reality or she has a better understanding of sex. Her sex fantasy has been satisfied and she needn’t to in her dreams. I interpret “It’s a pity that I am not able to experience it again though I’d like to genuinely” as it won’t be mysterious any more once sex fantasy turns into sex experience.

Therefore, dreaming of flying predicts the dreamer will become successful in the future.

在梦中，母亲的情绪一定经历了从焦虑、恐惧到虚惊一场的转变。这和现实又是多么接近啊。梦最大的价值之一，预示了现实中，我们将用怎样的心态面对各种处境和压力，一切都会回归平静，哪怕你现在焦头烂额。

# 11.梦见掉牙

【梦境】

我老做一个重复的梦，梦里自己的牙齿全掉了。现实中，本人牙确实不好。

\--网友留言

【用弗洛伊德的理论解梦】

【分析结论】 逃避或信念动摇

掉牙在潜意识中的象征意义是“生命力衰退”，牙齿具有攻击、活力、自尊、成长等多重意义，是阳刚和生存本能的反映。所以，掉牙的梦往往是消极、悲伤、焦虑等情绪的投射。

牙齿的生长是和一个人年龄成正比的，而掉牙在心理上代表着回到了没有牙齿的年龄，这在心理学上称为退行，象征着一个人在面对事情的时候，希望以小孩子的方式去应对。只有孩子才有权利任性、得到宽容和原谅，这也暗示了梦主可能遇到了难以处理的事情。

当然了掉牙还有一种解释，牙齿是我们人体中最坚固的东西，所以也是一种信念的象征。掉牙也象征着信念、价值观、人生观等思想正遭遇冲击，甚至发生动摇。另外，当事人本人也有牙病，生理的刺激也可能是梦的一个主要来源，有些掉牙的梦也可能是牙病的直接反应。

【建议】 理性地分析所面临的问题

生命中总会遇到各种情况，我们不能期待事事如意和顺心，成长就是学会处理各类问题。梦是一种知晓自己潜意识很好地通途，利用梦反映出的冲突和矛盾解决现实问题是非常有意义的。

建议当事人首先要把自己的牙病治疗好。其次，在遇到困扰的问题时，把自己的情绪延迟，更多用理性从各方面做分析，各种可能都整理出来。在这样的整理过程中，问题答案可能就已经出现了，本人的应变能力也会逐渐提升。

【周公解梦】

梦见自己的牙齿脱落，将会与人发生争吵

在休闲活动方面也会不顺，说得明白一点，就是水难之虞。

划船、冲浪、游泳等等的水上游乐应该全面禁止。

在进行其他游乐时也要特别小心。

【中医解梦】

中医认为，牙本肾，也就是说牙的根本是肾。肾并不是肾脏，而是一系列功能和器官的组合代表。梦见牙齿掉落，可能是因为肾有病症。

# 17.梦见去世的奶奶和我说话

【梦境】

我从小在奶奶家长大，所以和奶奶的感情很深。奶奶去世前，我有段时间总会做奶奶去世的梦，但奶奶那时身体并没有出现什么问题。奶奶是今年5月份去世的，可我最近梦里总会梦见她又和我说话，有时还在屋里忙东忙西的，非常真实的感觉。这样的梦让我总有些怅然若失。

\--男，30岁，职员，最近梦多，睡眠不太好

【用弗洛伊德的理论解梦】

【分析结论】

心理活动中有一种是预见心理，对没有发生的事情有一种预感和担心，而这种预感在意识层面常常是被理智屏蔽的，所以这种对未来的焦虑情绪就会被压抑进入潜意识。梦中，因为意识的暂时“休息”，潜意识中被压抑的焦虑就会以梦境体现出来。这一方面是焦虑的一种宣泄，同时也提示自己对预感的事作出一定的心理准备。
