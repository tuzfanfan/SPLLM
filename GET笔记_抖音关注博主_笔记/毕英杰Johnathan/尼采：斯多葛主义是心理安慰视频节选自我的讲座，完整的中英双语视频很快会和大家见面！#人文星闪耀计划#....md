## 摘要

💡 **尼采的核心观点**
- 尼采认为斯多葛主义是那些深受命运折磨之人的应对方式。
- 这些人完全失去了对外在世界的掌控。

📌 **斯多葛派的做法**
- 斯多葛派告诉这些人，诸如失去朋友、经历痛苦、缺乏快乐、丧失荣誉等外在事物都无关紧要。
- 因为他们认为外在的善是无关紧要的。

🧠 **人们接受斯多葛哲学的原因**
- 人们接受斯多葛哲学这一信条，并非基于第一性原理进行理性推导。
- 也不是因为它符合直觉，实际上它非常反直觉。
- 而是因为它在心理上有某种慰藉作用。


## 原文

Nietzsche's point is that stoicism ISA coping for people who have been so molested by fate, for people who have completely lost control of the external world and what the stoics do is that they goto these people and they say, look, none of this, none of this matters. You lost friends, you're experiencing pain, you don't have any pleasure, you lost your honor. None of this matters because external goods are indifference and nietzsche's point is that, that people latch onto this tenant of stoic philosophy not because they've reason to it from first principles, certainly not because it's intuitive, it's highly unintuitive, but because it's psychologically country.
