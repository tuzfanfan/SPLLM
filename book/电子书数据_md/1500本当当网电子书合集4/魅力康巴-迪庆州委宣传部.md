在地球“第三极”青藏高原东南部，有个广袤、美丽、富饶的地方叫“康巴”。

康巴地区泛指今天的云南省迪庆藏族自治州、青海省玉树藏族自治州、四川省甘孜藏族自治州及凉山彝族自治州木里县和西藏自治区的昌都地区，地域面积达50多万平方公里，人口约250万。

康巴有着无数的雪山、冰川、江河、湿地、湖泊等。

雪山富集。著名的神山有冈底斯山、念青唐古拉、格拉丹东、贡嘎山和卡瓦格博等等。

江河纵横。金沙江、澜沧江、怒江、雅砻江等发源或流经康巴大地，抚育着流域数亿人口，于是人们把它称作“亚洲水塔”。

草原辽阔。塔公、依拉等不胜枚举，是中国最重要湿地生态系统的组成部分。

湖泊众多。碧塔海、纳帕海、米格措、五须措等高原湖泊数不胜数，它们虽然没有青海湖般的体量，但柔美、秀丽却总是让摄影家放不下相机，让人流连忘返。

特殊的地理环境，养育了许多珍稀动植物种，是滇金丝猴、黑颈鹤、野牦牛、藏羚羊、云豹们的乐园，是银杏、红豆杉的家园，国家保护名录上的动植物就有数百种之多。

特殊的地理环境孕育了独特的康巴文化。早在4000年前，就有人类在这一带生存繁衍。迪庆的戈登遗址，证明了新石器时代就有人类活动的清晰痕迹，在同一时期，与昌都卡若遗址关系密切的文化地图还可以这样描绘：往东是有着同样葬俗的金沙江、雅砻江流域，往西是怒江流域的横断山脉古人，几乎都奉行石板葬俗。可见他们早就已经扎根于此，开发耕耘于此，神山和圣水崇拜也已经形成。

公元630年，康巴全境已经融入吐蕃的版图，这个时候是吐蕃历史上原来信奉的苯教和佛教相互较劲的时期，稍后佛教取得了王家的支持，占了主要地位。但后来又出现了大规模的灭佛事件，接着吐蕃分崩离析，雪域大地上部落林立，佛教也再次兴盛，许多经典传说就来自康巴地区，由此可见佛法再次中兴过程中，康巴人有着不可替代的作用。于是，佛法和佛号成为了镌刻在康巴大地上的一道风景线，影响了社会及人们生活的各个方面。

然而康巴文化又且是一个“佛”字了得！

近一千年以来，康巴大地上出现过许多惊人的科技创举，在云南境内的金沙江上，康巴人修建了第一座横跨长江的铁索桥，在康巴大地上林林总总的修建了许多土碉堡和石碉堡，历经数百年的风雨浸湿而兀立。

康巴人在藏医药学方面的贡献也是独特的，如心脏神药七十味珍珠丸，如列入中国十大民医的藏医大师向·此称江初活佛。

康巴人在文化艺术方面塑造的格萨尔系列人物形象更是吸引了人类千年的眼球。提到歌舞的海洋，人们就联想到玉树，说起情歌的故乡，人们会指出这是甘孜，一提起藏东明珠，人们就会自然而然说是昌都，谈到人类精神家园香格里拉，人们就会向往云南迪庆。

新中国成立以来，康巴大地改天换地，大事多多，喜事连连，中国第一个民族自治地州——四川省甘孜藏族自治州设立，接着又有青海省玉树藏族自治州、云南省迪庆藏族自治州成立，昌都地区也随着西藏自治区的成立建立。康巴人当家做了国家的主人，社会形态从封建农奴制跨越迈入社会主义社会，康巴融入了国家经济社会发展的轨道，古老大地焕发出勃勃生机。

特别是改革开放和实施西部大开发以来，康巴人发扬“敢叫日月换新天”的精神，逢山开路，遇水架桥，国民经济基础设施建设翻天覆地，日新月异。到了20世纪末期，康巴地区已基本实现县县通油路，村村通公路，神鹰翱翔蓝天，坐飞机已经不再是梦想，而只是出行的普通方式。

重视民生，享受幸福，是现代康巴的追求。孩子们免费上学，病人就医有保险，老人老有所养，居者有其屋，劳者有其业。曾被视为奢侈品的汽车、电视、手机等，甚至互联网也成为寻常农牧民人家的寻常事。

康巴地大物博，人文博大精深，盛世盛会连连。有诗为证：“四季枯荣皆斑斓，八方人文俱灿烂。”我们想尽力做到“天地舒展一卷中”，但康巴之大、康巴之美、康巴之深、康巴之奇，是怎样的文笔，怎样的镜头都无法囊括，在这里，我们只能采撷康巴自然和人文海洋中的点滴浪花，为您展示康巴山水、康巴风情、康巴盛事。

可能您没有时间亲临康巴，或者驻足康巴、体验康巴，但应该不吝少许时间翻阅这部忠实历史和现实的图册，它的精美，它的视角，它的意境，让您走进康巴，感受康巴，赞美康巴！

Southeast to the Earth's“third pole”，the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, is a vast, beautiful and richly-endowed area called“Khampa”.

Khampa region generally refers to a land area including today's Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture in Yunnan Province, Yushu Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture in Qinghai Province, Garzê Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture and Muli Tibetan Autonomous County of Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture in Sichuan Province, and Qamdo Prefecture in the Tibet Autonomous Region, totally covering more than 500，000 square kilometers with a population of around 2.5 million.

Khampa has numerous snow-capped mountains, glaciers, rivers, wetlands and lakes.

Snow-capped mountains：Well-known sacred mountains are Mount Kailash, Mount Nyainqêntanglha, The Geladaindong Peak, Mount Gongga, Kawakarpo Peak, etc.

Rivers：Rivers such as the Jinsha River, the Lancang River, the Nu River, and the Yalong River originate in or flow across the Khampa area, nourishing millions of people throughout their valleys. Therefore, people call the area“Asia's Water Tower”.

Grasslands：the Tagong, the Yila and other countless grasslands are components of the most important wetland ecosystems of China.

Lakes：Here are numerous plateau lakes like the Mibgai Co Lake, the Odkyi Tso Lake, the Bita Lake, and the Napa Lake. They are smaller than Qinghai Lake, but their graceful beauty constantly keeps photographers enchanted and travelers attracted.Their picturesque scenery renders those＆nbsp；

The unique geographic environment is home to various rare animals and plants, such as the Yunnan snub-nosed monkey, the black-necked crane, wild yak, the Tibetan antelope, the clouded leopard, ginkgo, Chinese yew, etc, among which hundreds are on the national protection list.

Unique Khampa culture is nurtured in this unique geographic environment. Human beings started to reside the region 4，000 years ago.The evidence to such early human activities in the Neolithic age is the Gedeng ruin in Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture.Several cultures of the same period are closely linked to Qamdo Karub Relics, and the cultural map can be depicted as：To the east are the Jinsha River and the Yalong River valleys where people shared the same funeral rites；to the west are the Nu River valleys where most, if not all, ancient people of Hengduan Mountains followed a stone slab burial custom.Those ruins are signs that people have long lived and farmed here, and have already developed worships of sacred mountains and holly waters.

By 630 A. D.，the entire Khampa region had become a territory of the Tubo.It was a time when indigenous Bon and Buddhism tried to overpower each other.With the royal support, Buddhism later gained the upper hand.Then came large-scale events of abolishing Buddhism, followed by the collapse of the Kingdom.Numerous tribes of the region, however, carried on the religion and helped Buddhism to thrive again, leaving behind classic legends and stories.Khampa people thus played an indispensible role in the course of Buddhism's resurgence.Buddhist doctrines＆nbsp；and Buddha's names have already become a part of Khampa scenery and have penetrated every aspect of Khampa society and daily life.

Khampa culture, however, is much more than Buddhism.

Many astonishing scientific and technical miracles have been made in the recent millennium. Over the Yunnan section of the Jinsha River, Khampa people built he first chain bridge across the Yangtze.They also built a large number of earth blockhouses and stone blockhouses, which still keep standing after centuries of winds and rains.

Khampa people have also made great contribution to Tibetan medicine and pharmacology. To name a few, the miracle-working cardiac medicine“70-Flavor Pearl Bolus，”and a master of Tibetan medicine, Living Buddha Hsiang Chengchu Jiangchu, who is listed in“the Top Ten Folk Doctors in China”.

In literature and arts, Khampa people have created the Gesar characters, which have been appealing to people for thousands of years. At the mention of a“sea of songs and dances，”one would think of Yushu；mentioning“the hometown of love songs，”one would point out it's Garzê；speaking of the“eastern-Tibet pearl，”one would naturally say it’s Qamdo；while it comes to the mankind’s spiritual garden, Shangri-La, one would long for Diqing in Yunnan.

Tremendous changes have taken place, one after another, in Khampa since the founding of New China. Following the establishment of the first minority autonomous prefecture—Sichuan Garzê Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, other prefectures—Qinghai Yushu Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, Yunnan Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, and Qamdo Prefecture along with the Tibet Autonomous Region—were set up in succession.Khampa people rose to be masters of their own country.Feudal serfdom was abolished and socialism was built.Khampa was back on track to integrate into national＆nbsp；economic development.The ancient land started to shine with its vigor and vitality.

Since the beginning of reform and opening to the outside world, and with the implementation of“large-scale development of the western regions”strategy, Khampa people displayed the spirit of bold confidence and hard struggle, cutting paths through the mountains and building bridges across the rivers. Infrastructure constructions for national economic development were going on in full swing, changing the old Khampa dramatically and rapidly.By the end of last century, Khampa had turned into a region where asphalt roads reach all its counties and highways reach all its villages.Travelling by air was no longer dreams, but merely a common way of transportation.

Now Khampa people attach great importance to people's quality of life and happiness is their pursuit. Kids now attend school for free；patients now have medical insurance；people now have houses and jobs.Things like cars, televisions, cell phones and even the Internet that once seen as luxuries now become commonplace for ordinary farmer or herdsman families.

Khampa is vast in territory, rich in natural resources, extensive in culture, and profound in arts. In a grand time of peace and prosperity there are many grand gatherings and ceremonies.A poems goes，“Beautiful it is in all seasons, glorious they are from all regions.”We wish we could“convey all its looks in a book，”but the breadth, beauty, depth and mystery of Khampa are beyond any words or photos.Here we can only present you a very limited selection of Khampa nature and culture, to show some Khampa sceneries, customs and ceremonies.

You may not have the time to visit Khampa in person, or have a short stay there and experience it, but you should spare a little time to read this photo pamphlet. With its loyalty to history and facts, its delicacy, perspective and conception, it may well give you a chance to approach Khampa, feel Khampa, and laud Khampa！

康巴地区地处青藏高原与云贵高原和川西台地过渡地带，雪山雄伟林立，江河自北向南纵穿全境，高原湖泊星星点点，草原星罗棋布，森林茂密参天，生态保持完整。在区域内，仅占中国国土面积不到0.4%，却拥有全国20%以上高等植物的“三江并流”世界自然遗产区。作为康巴地区的缩影，迪庆州森林覆盖率达73.9%，拥有含我国特有动物滇金丝猴在内的77种国家级保护动物和国家级重点保护珍稀濒危植物34种，拥有37种省级保护植物，是世界生物多样性最丰富的地区之一。

Situated in the transitional zone between Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau and the western Sichuan tableland, Khampa region retains well-preserved ecosystems with snow-capped mountains standing majestically in great numbers, rivers running north-south through the entire region, plateau lakes scattered across, grasslands spreading all over, and forests dense with giant trees. Khampa region covers less than 0.4%of the national territory, but has the Three Parallel Rivers UNESCO world heritage site that is home to more than 20%of the higher plant species in China.As a miniature of Khampa, Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture maintains a forest coverage rate of 73.9%.Living here are 34 national key protected rare and endangered plants，37 provincial protected plants, and 77 national protected animals including the Yunnan snub-nosed monkey, which is found only in China.It is one of the most biologically diverse regions on earth.

德钦梅里雪山（卡瓦格博）

Meili Snow Mountain, Diqing

泽仁平措 摄

Photo by Zeren Pingcuo

卡瓦格博藏语意为“白色的雪山”，是梅里雪山主峰，海拔6740米，是云南省最高峰，是藏传佛教朝觐圣地八大神山之一，常年有大批西藏、青海、四川、甘肃等地和来自印度、尼泊尔的香客千里迢迢赶来朝拜、转经。卡瓦格博峰山麓分布有明永冰川和雨崩瀑布等景观。该峰同时也是人类至今未登顶的处女峰。

Kawagebo means“white snow mountain”in Tibetan. It's the main peak of Meili Snow Mountain, rising 6，740 meters above the sea level, the tallest peak in Yunan Province.As one of the eight sacred mountains for Tibetan Buddhism, all the year round, large numbers of pilgrims from Tibet, Qinghai, Sichuan, Gansu, even India and Nepal visit it for pilgrimage and spinning prayer wheels.Sights at its piedmont are the Mingyong Glacier, the Avalanche Snowfall, etc.It is also still a virgin peak whose summit no one, so far, has reached.

香格里拉纳帕海

The Napa Lake, Shangri-La

蔺以光 摄

Photo by Lin Yiguang

位于香格里拉县城北郊，面积3434公顷，属低纬度、高海拔湿地，由草甸、沼泽、水面和湖周森林构成的喀斯特型季节性沼泽湿地，是国家一级保护动物黑颈鹤的重要越冬地。

Located in the north suburbs of Shangri-La County, the 3，434 hectares Napa Lake is low in latitude but high in altitude. It is a seasonal Karst wetland comprised of water meadows, swamps, water bodies, and lakeside forests.The lake area is an important habitat for the black-necked crane, a first-class state protected bird, who comes here to live through the winter.

An Autumn View of the Ningji Lake

The Ningji Lake is located at an altitude of 4，200 meters in Shanglaxiu Township of Yushu County. Its water body occupies more than 90 square kilometers, with its deepest point at approximately 30 meters.It's a highly mineralized salt water lake abundant in plateau naked carp.Every spring and summer, various birds, including bar-headed geese, Pallas's gulls, ruddy shelducks, whooper swans and black-necked cranes, hatch their eggs and raise their young here.

迪庆普达措国家公园（碧塔海）

The Potatso National Park（the Bita Hai Lake），Diqing

普达措是中国内地第一个国家公园，位于云南省迪庆州香格里拉县城东，2007年正式开放。“普达措”藏语意为“与神同舟渡达彼岸”。主要由碧塔海、属都湖、属都牧场等组成，有国家重点保护的高山植物和重唇鱼等。

The Potatso National Park, opened to the public in 2007，is the first national park in mainland China. It is located in the east of Shangri-La County, Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture.In Tibetan，“potatso”means to“sail to the other bank with the God.”Mainly composed of the Bita Hai Lake, the Shudu Lake and the Shudu Pasture, it is home to various national key protected plateau plants and lip fish.

虎跳峡

Tiger Leaping Gorge

Tiger Leaping Gorge is situated in Tiger Leaping Township of Shangri-La, Diqing Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture. The gorge has three sections—the Upper Leap, the Middle Leap, and the Lower Leap—and 18 rapids.With the upper gorge entrance at the aptitude of 1，800 meters and the lower 1，630 meters, the gorge has a drop height of 170 meters.It is one of the world's best-known gorges.
